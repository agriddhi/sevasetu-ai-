ℹ️ About SevaSetu — how it works & data sources

How it works: Citizens submit development requests by voice, text or photo in any Indian language. Google Gemini transcribes, detects the language, translates, classifies category & urgency (with a reason), geolocates the district and scores sentiment. Reports aggregate into district hotspots via a transparent composite index, and Gemini writes ranked, scheme-mapped project recommendations for national policymakers.

Priority formula: 40% demand volume + 20% urgency + 20% state infrastructure-deficit proxy + 10% 24-hour momentum + 10% population equity. Every component is visible in the dashboard — fully auditable.

Data sources: Request themes modeled on public grievance patterns (data.gov.in, CPGRAMS); infrastructure-deficit proxies based on NITI Aayog SDG India Index; district populations from Census of India 2011. Demo records are synthetic; no personal data is stored.

Digital Public Good principles: open & auditable, privacy-first (no PII), multilingual & voice-first (22-language vision), scalable state-by-state via configuration. Google stack: Gemini (intake, vision, recommendations) · Google Maps (hotspot map) · Firebase Firestore (cloud backend) · Google Cloud hosting.
